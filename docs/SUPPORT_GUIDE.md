# DxCon Support Guide — Enterprise v1.0

## Support Center

The Support Center module (`/healthcare-ecosystem/support-center`) tracks support channels and SLA tiers for commercial customers.

## Support Channels

| Channel | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| Email | General inquiries, account issues |
| Ticket | Production incidents, integration blockers |
| Knowledge Base | Self-service how-to and troubleshooting |

## SLA Tiers

| Tier | Response target | Scope |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| PILOT | Best effort | Demo / evaluation |
| PROFESSIONAL | 8 business hours | Single-site production |
| ENTERPRISE | 4 business hours | Multi-site + federation |

## Escalation Path

1. Check [Knowledge Base](healthcare-ecosystem/knowledge-base) and role guides at `/user-guides`.
2. Review [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).
3. For production outages: Monitoring Center → Incident Runbook.
4. For security/PHI: Security & Compliance hub.
