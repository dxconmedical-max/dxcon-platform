# DxCon Customer Guide — Enterprise v1.0

## Who This Is For

Clinic administrators, lab directors, and operations managers deploying DxCon for diagnostic workflows.

## Getting Started

1. Log in with credentials provided by your DxCon administrator.
2. Open the **Healthcare Ecosystem** hub at `/healthcare-ecosystem` (admin) for module overview.
3. Configure your site via Multi-Tenant hub at `/multi-tenant` (enterprise tenants).
